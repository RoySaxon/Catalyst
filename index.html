Your data sheet (TDS) optional

What is this?

Every resin is a little different. The technical data sheet (TDS) that comes with it tells you how much catalyst to use at a certain temperature, and usually how long it takes to gel.

Copy those numbers in here and the app works from your resin instead of a typical one. If the sheet says 1.5% at 20 °C, gel time 25 minutes, type 1.5, 20 and 25.

If the sheet only gives a percentage, fill in the first two boxes and leave gel time empty. If you don't have the sheet, leave everything blank: the app uses typical values and marks them as estimates.

Each product keeps its own numbers, so a gelcoat sheet won't change your laminating resin.

    1. What this app is

    Laminator's Catalyst Calculator is an estimating aid for people trained to work with composite materials. It is in beta and still being tested. It does not replace the manufacturer's technical data sheet (TDS) or safety data sheet (SDS), your training, your workplace's procedures or your supervisor's instructions.

    2. The figures are estimates

    Catalyst ratios, amounts, gel times, working times, pot lives and conversions are calculated from typical workshop values, from published data for specific named products, and from a mathematical model. Figures marked "Estimate" have no published figure behind them. Real results vary with the product and its batch, age and storage, the promoter level, the catalyst grade, the amount mixed, the shape of the part, the temperature of the materials and the mould, humidity, and how accurately things are measured. Results can differ from those shown by a third or more.

    3. The manufacturer comes first

    Where this app and the manufacturer's TDS, SDS or instructions differ, follow the manufacturer. Use only the catalyst or hardener the manufacturer specifies for the product. Test a small batch before committing a part, especially with a new batch of resin or catalyst.

    4. Safety

    Catalysts such as MEKP are hazardous. They can cause blindness, serious burns and fire. Mixed resin gives off heat as it cures, and a large or over-catalysed batch can overheat, smoke and ignite. Wear the protective equipment the SDS requires, work in adequate ventilation, and follow your workplace's emergency procedures. Safety guidance in this app is general information only and does not replace the SDS or professional advice. In an emergency in Australia, call 000.
